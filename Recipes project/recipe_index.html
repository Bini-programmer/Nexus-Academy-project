Description for Burgers

A juicy, fire-grilled beef patty stacked with fresh tomatoes, crisp lettuce, raw onions, and pickles, finished with mayo and ketchup in a toasted sesame bun. Served alongside hot, golden-crisp French fries for the ultimate classic meal.

Ingredients

For the Burgers (Serves 2)

  • 2 sesame seed burger buns, split
  • 2 ground beef patties (approx. ¼ lb each)
  • 4 slices fresh tomato
  • 4 rings raw white onion
  • 4-6 slices dill pickles
  • 2 large leaves fresh green leaf lettuce
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp ketchup

    • For the Side

  • 2 cups frozen or fresh-cut potato French fries
  • Cooking oil (for frying)
  • Salt, to taste

Steps to make the burger:

  1. Prepare the French Fries: Heat cooking oil in a deep fryer or heavy pan to 375°F (190°C). Fry the potatoes for 4 to 6 minutes until golden brown and crispy, then drain on paper towels and toss with salt while hot.
  2. Season and Cook the Patties: Heat a skillet or grill to medium-high. Season both sides of the beef patties generously with salt and black pepper. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes per side until fully browned and cooked through.
  3. Toast the Buns: Place the sesame seed buns cut-side down on the skillet for 1 to 2 minutes until lightly toasted.
  4. Assemble the Bottom Layer: Spread mayonnaise evenly on the bottom buns. Place a cooked beef patty on each, followed by a layer of pickle slices and a squeeze of ketchup.
  5. Add the Fresh Veggies: Layer on the white onion rings, tomato slices, and fresh green leaf lettuce leaves.
  6. Finish and Serve: Cover with the top sesame buns and serve immediately alongside the hot crispy fries.
